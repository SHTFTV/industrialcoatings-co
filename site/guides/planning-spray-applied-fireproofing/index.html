Planning spray-applied fireproofing work

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · Updated September 28, 2026

The project information needed to coordinate a specification-led passive fire-protection scope.

Confirm the assembly

The design listing, material, required thickness, substrate and primer compatibility must be established from the project requirements.

Coordinate readiness

Steel should be accessible and in the required condition. Other trades, enclosures, services and finished surfaces affect masking, sequencing and access.

Control the work zone

Spray application requires a managed area with suitable ventilation, protection and restricted access. Project-specific safety planning belongs with qualified professionals.

Plan quality review

Thickness, adhesion or density checks may be required by the specification or authority. Define responsibilities and documentation before application begins.

Safety note

Protective coatings and spray-applied materials can involve hazardous preparation, chemicals, specialized equipment and controlled work zones. This guide supports scope discussions; it is not an application manual.

Use a coordinated element schedule

Connect the specified protection to structural elements and current drawing references. Include access constraints and interfaces with services. Ask the design team to resolve gaps and conflicting requirements before mobilisation, and keep accepted changes available to the installer and inspector.

Protect the completed work

Agree how subsequent trades report disturbance and who arranges review and repair. Schedule inspection access before finishes or services conceal treated areas. Retain the final assembly references, located inspection records and repair closeout in the owner’s handover folder.
