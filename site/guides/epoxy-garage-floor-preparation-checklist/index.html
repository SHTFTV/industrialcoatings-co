Epoxy Garage Floor Preparation Checklist

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · Updated September 28, 2026

A condition-led checklist for moisture, contamination, cracks, profiling, joints and cure planning.

Start with evidence

Record the existing substrate, exposure, damage, access and previous work. Photographs are useful when they are labelled as actual projects or clearly identified as representative.

Define performance and constraints

Identify the required outcome, operating hours, adjacent finishes, ventilation, cure or drying time and documentation obligations before selecting a product.

Request a written scope

The proposal should identify preparation, repairs, materials, application limits, exclusions, protection and the party responsible for inspections or testing.

Verify before closeout

Review appearance, coverage, transitions and required records. Keep product and maintenance information with the property file.

Record the existing slab honestly

Show cracks, joints, previous coatings and areas exposed to oil or other contamination. Ask the contractor how moisture and substrate condition will be assessed for the proposed system. Do not assume a sound-looking surface is ready for coating, or that a decorative finish will correct a moving joint.

Compare complete preparation scopes

Request the proposed preparation method, repair approach and treatment of edges and joints in writing. Identify disposal, dust control and protection responsibilities. Agree how the prepared surface is reviewed before coating and how the garage may be used during the system’s return-to-service period.
