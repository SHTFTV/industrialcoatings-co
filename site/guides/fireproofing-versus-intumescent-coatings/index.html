Fireproofing vs. Intumescent Coatings

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · Updated September 28, 2026

The visible and technical differences between cementitious fireproofing and reactive intumescent coatings.

Start with evidence

Record the existing substrate, exposure, damage, access and previous work. Photographs are useful when they are labelled as actual projects or clearly identified as representative.

Define performance and constraints

Identify the required outcome, operating hours, adjacent finishes, ventilation, cure or drying time and documentation obligations before selecting a product.

Request a written scope

The proposal should identify preparation, repairs, materials, application limits, exclusions, protection and the party responsible for inspections or testing.

Verify before closeout

Review appearance, coverage, transitions and required records. Keep product and maintenance information with the property file.

Compare the design basis first

Ask the designer to identify the required assembly, structural element and exposure conditions before considering alternatives. A thin-looking finish and a textured spray are not interchangeable simply because both are described as fire protection. Obtain the relevant system evidence and review any substitution through the project process.

Compare installation and maintenance constraints

Record surface preparation, primers, finish requirements, access and inspection responsibilities for each option. Ask how future damage is assessed and repaired. Keep technical acceptance separate from appearance approval so an attractive sample does not inadvertently authorise an unsupported system change.
