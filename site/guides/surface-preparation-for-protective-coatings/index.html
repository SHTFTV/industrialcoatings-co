Practical guide

Surface preparation before protective coating

Why cleaning, profiling and environmental control carry as much weight as the coating itself.

01

Identify the substrate

Concrete, carbon steel, galvanized steel and previously painted surfaces require different assessment and preparation. Unknown coatings may need compatibility testing.

02

Remove what should not remain

Oil, salts, dust, loose rust, weak concrete and poorly bonded paint can defeat adhesion. The preparation method should match the specified system and site constraints.

03

Measure conditions

Surface temperature, air temperature, relative humidity, dew point and concrete moisture can affect application. Readings should be considered against the manufacturer’s limits.

04

Treat edges and interruptions

Welds, sharp edges, bolts, cracks, joints and penetrations need deliberate detailing. Broad-field application cannot compensate for missed details.

Safety note

Protective coatings and spray-applied materials can involve hazardous preparation, chemicals, specialized equipment and controlled work zones. This guide supports scope discussions; it is not an application manual.
