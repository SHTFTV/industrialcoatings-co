HiBar Fireproofing Repairs

Fireproofing repair restores a specified assembly; it is not a cosmetic patch.

Identify the existing system

Confirm the product or approved repair system, substrate, primer, design listing and required thickness before material is installed.

Map damage and causes

Document impact, water exposure, delamination, service penetrations and areas removed by later trades.

Verify the repair

Preparation, bonding treatment, thickness checks, curing and inspection should follow the project specification and responsible authority.

Start with the existing conditions
