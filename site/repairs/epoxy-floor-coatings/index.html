Repair page

Epoxy Floor Coating Repairs

A durable repair begins by separating coating failure from movement, moisture or concrete deterioration.

Read the failure pattern

Peeling, bubbles, tyre pickup, cracks and worn traffic lanes point to different causes and preparation needs.

Test the substrate

Moisture, contamination, weak concrete and incompatible prior coatings can defeat a new patch.

Plan the visual transition

Local repair edges, colour, flake broadcast and gloss may remain visible. Agree on the acceptable blend or full recoating area first.

Start with the existing conditions

Send the substrate, exposure, access constraints and desired outcome to build@buildershaus.com.
