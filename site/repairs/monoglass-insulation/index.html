Monoglass Insulation Repairs

A Monoglass repair should address the cause of damage and match the required performance and exposed finish.

Diagnose before patching

Check moisture, leaks, impact, substrate failure and previous coatings before adding new material.

Confirm compatibility

Existing product, thickness, colour, texture and current manufacturer guidance affect the repair material and preparation.

Blend and document

Use a sample where appearance matters, protect adjacent work and record the repaired areas and conditions.

Start with the existing conditions

Send the substrate, exposure, access constraints and desired outcome to build@buildershaus.com.
