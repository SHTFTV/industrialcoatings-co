Industrial Coatings: Legacy Applications and Current Scope

Recovered coating experience is useful evidence, but every new floor, wall or equipment coating starts with today’s substrate and exposure.

Garage and commercial floors

Garage, retail and light-industrial floors need preparation matched to concrete condition, moisture, traffic, chemicals, slip resistance and the desired finish.

Stores and operating facilities

Phasing, odour, cure time, shutdown windows and customer access often control system choice as much as colour or gloss.

Other applications

Cabinet resurfacing, protective coatings and specialty spray work should be described accurately and separated from insulation and fireproofing evidence.
