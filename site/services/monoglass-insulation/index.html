Monoglass Spray-Applied Insulation

Monoglass is a spray-applied insulation and acoustic treatment. The material, receiving surface and finished appearance all need to be evaluated together.

Where the system fits

Common applications include exposed ceilings, irregular substrates and spaces needing continuous thermal or acoustic treatment. Suitability depends on the current product documentation and assembly.

Preparation and application

The substrate must be sound, compatible and prepared as required. Material storage, water, equipment, overspray control and access affect the plan.

Finish expectations

Colour, texture, thickness and exposed appearance should be agreed before production. Representative images do not replace a sample area or manufacturer requirements.
