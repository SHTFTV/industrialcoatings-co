HiBar Spray-Applied Fireproofing

HiBar is treated here as a spray-applied fire-resistive system for structural protection—not as crawlspace insulation.

Preserve the tested assembly

Product identity, substrate, primer compatibility, required thickness and the listed assembly must be confirmed from current project documents. A trade name alone is not enough to select or repair a fireproofing system.

Application planning

Steel readiness, masking, access, ventilation, sequencing and quality-control responsibilities belong in the scope before spraying begins.

Existing HiBar installations

Older work should be assessed for impact damage, moisture exposure, bond loss, missing material and undocumented alterations before a repair method is chosen.

Start with the existing conditions
